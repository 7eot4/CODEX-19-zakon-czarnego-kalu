THE RULE / OATH OF THE NIGHT CHAPTER

"O to słowo z Gdańska!" - our maxim and guiding principle.

Honor the rules.
Challenge dogma.

We swear by the Black Seal: to maintain brotherhood, not to kneel before other people's hypocrisy and not to confuse dark aesthetics with a lack of principles. Our necromancy resurrects dead memes. Our gay pride does not ask for absolution.

From the abyss, with due perspective,
The ZCK Chapter

  1. I

    Follow the rule, not someone else's shame

    Zakon Czarnego Kału stands for freedom, loyalty and consent. Dignity needs no permission from self-appointed guardians of morality.

  2. II

    Resurrect humour. Bury prejudice.

    A necromancer can raise a skeleton. They need not raise the level of debate with someone who sees another person’s love as a threat.

  3. III

    Brotherhood without coercion

    Someone else’s “no” ends the ritual. The title of Grand Master gives no power over another person’s identity, boundaries or heart.
